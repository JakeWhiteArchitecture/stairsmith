A staircase calculator that thinks in 3D

StairSmith is a free online staircase design tool from Jake White Architecture. Set your floor-to-floor height, stair width and going, choose a straight, single winder or double winder layout, and watch a complete timber staircase — treads, risers, kite winders, stringers, newel posts and balustrade — rebuild instantly in 3D. It is built for architects, designers, joiners and self-builders who need reliable early-stage stair geometry fast.

✔Live 3D preview

Every parameter change rebuilds the full staircase model in real time, with measure and dimension tools in the viewport.

✔True winder geometry

Single and double winder turns are set out using the traditional four-step newel construction, with correct kite winders and post housings.

✔Building Regs checks

Rise, going, pitch, 2R+G, width and winder goings are checked live against Approved Document K guidance for private dwellings in England & Wales.

✔BIM & CAD export

Download an IFC4 model for BIM coordination, or a DXF with dimensioned plan, four elevations and stepped section views.

How it works

1

Set your parameters

Enter floor-to-floor height, stair width, going and newel size, then choose straight, single winder (90°) or double winder (180°) layout.

2

Check compliance as you design

The Building Regulations panel updates live, flagging anything outside Approved Document K guidance — maximum rise, minimum going, 42° pitch, the 2R+G rule and winder goings.

3

Export and verify

Download IFC or DXF files and have every output checked by a competent person before it is used — StairSmith is a preliminary design aid, not a substitute for professional judgement.

Frequently asked questions

Is StairSmith free to use?

Yes. StairSmith is a free, browser-based staircase design tool. There is nothing to install and no account is required.

Which staircase types can StairSmith design?

Straight flights, single winder (90° turn) and double winder (180° / U-shaped) timber staircases — including kite winders, newel posts, stringers, handrails, baserails and spindles.

Does StairSmith check UK Building Regulations?

StairSmith checks rise, going, pitch, 2R+G, stair width and winder geometry against guidance in Approved Document K (England and Wales, private dwellings). The checks are indicative only and must be verified by a competent person — other building types and jurisdictions have different requirements.

What file formats does StairSmith export?

IFC4 models for BIM workflows, and DXF drawings containing a dimensioned plan view, four orthographic elevations and section views cut through each flight.

Can I build directly from StairSmith outputs?

No. StairSmith is a preliminary design aid only. All outputs must be checked and verified by a suitably competent person before being relied upon or used for construction, manufacture, costing or regulatory submission.
